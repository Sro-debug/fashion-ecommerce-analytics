Fashion E-commerce Analytics

Business-analytics case study on a Myntra-style fashion marketplace: where growth is profitable, where revenue leaks, and which customers and channels are worth paying for.

Key findings and what to do about them

Every number below is computed from the SQL queries in this repo, not typed in by hand.

Growth and unit economics

Volume is not the problem. Margin in sale months is: contribution margin drops about ten points each time a big sale runs.

Categories and discounting

Women's ethnic wear leads on both GMV and return rate. Beyond 50% off, returns rise and margin collapses.

Sale events

Each event is compared with the 30 non-sale days before it, so business growth is not counted as sale lift.

Customers and acquisition

Retention cohorts, RFM segments and channel quality.

Operations: payment mode, geography and returns

Cash-on-delivery in smaller cities is where cancellations and return-to-origin shipments concentrate.
