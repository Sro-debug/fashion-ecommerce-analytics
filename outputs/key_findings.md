# Key findings

_All figures computed from the synthetic dataset by `build_report.py`._

## 1. Sales add ~204% more orders but only ~15% more contribution

**Sale economics.** Across 7 sale windows, median daily orders rose 204% against the prior 30 non-sale days, but average discount went from 37% to 50% and contribution margin fell from 32% to 14%. Contribution per day moved from 0% to +82% depending on the event.

**Action:** Judge sales on contribution per day, not GMV. Replace blanket discounts with targeted offers (members, larger baskets) and cap depth on low-margin brands. Pull-forward demand and post-sale dips are not measured here.

## 2. Items discounted 60%+ earn 2.2% margin vs 34.9% under 20% off

**Discounting.** The 60%+ band is 11.9% of items, returns 25.7% of the time (vs 21.2% under 20% off) and contributes ₹9 per item against ₹394. Returns step up at 50% off and above.

**Action:** Set a hard floor on contribution per item, flag SKUs that routinely sell at 60%+, and renegotiate cost or retire them.

## 3. Women Ethnic Wear returns 32.7% of delivered items; size and fit drives 46% of apparel returns

**Returns.** Overall item return rate is 21.9%. Footwear and western wear also sit above 25%. Cutting size/fit returns by one fifth would lower the overall rate by about 1.9 points.

**Action:** Prioritise size guidance and fit recommendations in ethnic wear and footwear, and track return rate by SKU and size, not just by category.

## 4. Tier 3 COD orders lose 17.3% to cancellation or RTO vs 4.1% for prepaid

**Payment and geography.** Contribution per order is ₹258 for Tier 3 COD vs ₹375 prepaid (31% lower). Closing half of the COD gap in Tier 2 and 3 would be worth about ₹6.5 lakh over the period.

**Action:** Offer a prepaid incentive, add OTP or WhatsApp confirmation before dispatch for COD, and restrict COD on high-risk pincodes or very deep-discount baskets.

## 5. Referral buyers are worth 1.8x Paid Social buyers

**Acquisition.** 90-day repeat: 45.5% vs 26.6%. Contribution per buyer: ₹1,394 vs ₹773. With illustrative CAC assumptions, contribution covers CAC 5.6x for Referral and only 1.7x for Paid Social.

**Action:** Shift budget toward referral and CRM, and optimise paid social for repeat propensity rather than first-order volume. CAC figures are assumptions, so replace them with real spend.

## 6. Champions and Loyal are 26% of customers but 58% of revenue

**Customer base.** At Risk customers (6.2% of buyers, average 195 days since last order) still hold 10.5% of revenue. Hibernating customers are 37.2% of buyers for 16.6% of revenue, and 22.6% are new or promising one-timers.

**Action:** Run win-back offers for At Risk first, since they were valuable. Nudge New / Promising buyers toward a second order within 30 to 60 days.

## 7. Private label earns 30.5% margin vs 24.5% for national brands, despite deeper discounts

**Private label.** Private label is 41% of GMV with 52% average discount (national brands: 32%), but its lower cost base absorbs it. Its return rate is higher (23.4% vs 20.4%).

**Action:** Keep growing private label where return rates are manageable, and treat the margin gap with care: category mix differs between brand types.
